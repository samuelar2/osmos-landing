Manage your payment method

osmos makes purchases on your behalf using the payment card you saved — trains, hotels, flights, and items you ask it to buy. Each purchase is either approved by you in the app, or made automatically within the spending limits you set. Amounts vary per purchase and are always shown before checkout.

Update, replace, or remove your card

  1. Open the osmos app on your iPhone.
  2. Go to Apps → Wallet.
  3. Under Payment, choose Replace to save a different card, or Remove to revoke it entirely.

Removing your card immediately stops all future charges, including automatic purchases. You can also change or switch off automatic purchase limits under Limits on the same screen.

Open in the osmos app

Need help?

If you can't access the app, email support@meetosmos.com from your account email and we'll remove the card for you.
