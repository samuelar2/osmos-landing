osmos Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 April 2026 Last updated: 29 July 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to osmos (the "App"). osmos is an AI-powered personal assistant application for iOS that helps you manage your daily life by connecting to the services you already use. This Privacy Policy explains how Brightboard AI LTD ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you use our App and related services (collectively, the "Services").

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the practices described herein, please do not use the Services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Account information: When you create an osmos account we collect your name and email address. Sign-in is handled by Apple, or by a password you set which we never see or store in readable form.
  • Chat input: Messages and prompts you submit to the AI assistant, including text you type and any files or photos you attach.
  • Preferences and settings: Your configuration choices, including tone, habit routines, reminder settings, and notification preferences.
  • Payment information: If you subscribe to a paid plan (e.g. Plus or Pro), payment details are processed by our third-party payment provider. We do not store full payment card numbers on our servers.

2.2 Information from connected services

osmos's core value is integrating with your existing tools. When you connect a third-party service, we access only the data necessary to provide the requested functionality.

Background scanning: osmos is a proactive assistant. Once you connect an account, osmos scans it continuously in the background — not only when you ask — to surface bookings and tickets, flag messages that need a reply, and prepare your briefings. This includes your email, calendar, and recent Slack and WhatsApp messages. Connecting an account is your instruction to do this; disconnecting it (Tool Manager, anytime) stops the scanning and removes the cached data for that service. You can also exclude an individual source from background scanning — without disconnecting it — in Routines → Pulse → Sources; osmos will then only read it when you ask.

For directly connected mailboxes and calendars this also deletes the authorisation itself; for apps connected through our integration partner, the grant remains registered with them until you delete your account, and you can also revoke it in that service's own settings.

2.3 Information collected automatically

  • Location: If you allow it, osmos records your device's approximate location when it changes significantly — including while the App is in the background — converts it to a street, neighbourhood and city, and stores that alongside the coordinates on your profile. It is used to make briefings and suggestions reflect where you are, and is included in the context sent to the AI. You can turn this off at any time in Settings, which also clears the stored location.
  • Device and usage data: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, crash logs, and general usage analytics (e.g. features used, session duration).
  • Usage statistics: Aggregated interaction counts displayed in the Usage panel (e.g. weekly interaction totals) are computed locally or server-side to show you your activity.
  • Log data: Server logs that may include your IP address, request timestamps, and API endpoints accessed.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and operate the Services: Processing your chat messages, executing actions across connected apps, delivering morning briefings, running habit routines, and surfacing relevant information from your connected accounts.
  • To personalise your experience: Tailoring AI responses based on your preferences, prior interactions, and connected account data.
  • To improve the Services: Analysing usage patterns — which features are used, when, and whether they worked — to improve features, fix bugs, and develop new functionality. These events are linked to your account but never contain your messages, emails, or documents.
  • To communicate with you: Sending transactional notifications (e.g. morning briefings, habit reminders, payment approvals) and, where you have opted in, marketing communications.
  • To ensure security and prevent abuse: Detecting and preventing fraud, unauthorised access, and other harmful activities.
  • To comply with legal obligations: Responding to lawful requests from regulatory authorities and law enforcement.

4. AI Processing and Automated Decision-Making

osmos uses artificial intelligence to understand your requests, draft communications, summarise information, and take actions on your behalf. When you ask osmos to perform a task (e.g. draft an email reply, create a calendar event, or send a Slack message), the AI processes the relevant context from your connected accounts to generate a response or action.

  • Machines, not people: Your chats, emails, and connected-service data are processed by automated systems. Nobody at Brightboard reads them in the normal operation of the service; direct production access is restricted to essential operations — debugging an issue you report, or investigating abuse — and we are progressively locking such access behind per-record, logged decryption.
  • Encryption at rest: Everything we store is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+). Sensitive personal data — such as travel documents, connected-account credentials, and cached message content — is additionally encrypted at the application layer with keys unique to your account, so those fields stay unreadable to anyone holding only a copy of the database. Other data — your chat history, the email details we mirror, your tasks and notifications — is stored in readable form, protected by disk-level encryption and access controls rather than per-account keys. This is not end-to-end encryption: we hold the keys, so we are technically able to decrypt what we store, and we restrict and log that access rather than making it impossible.
  • Your mailbox stays yours: We never see or store your email password. Where a mailbox is connected through our integration partner, the access token stays with them; where you connect Gmail or Outlook directly, we hold the token ourselves, encrypted with a key unique to your account. We do not keep a full copy of your mailbox, but we do mirror the details of your recent inbox — sender, recipients, subject, a short preview and labels — for up to 180 days so the Inbox and your briefings work, and we cache message bodies for a few days. Beyond that we keep the useful result: a ticket, a calendar event, a needs-reply flag.
  • AI subprocessors: To generate responses and run automations, the content a task needs is processed transiently by our AI infrastructure providers, under agreements or routing constraints that stop them retaining your data or training on it. A current list of subprocessor categories is available on request.
  • Product analytics: We use PostHog (hosted in the EU) to understand how osmos is used — event names, timestamps, and technical metadata only. We do not send message content, email content, or documents to our analytics provider, and analytics data is never used for advertising.
  • Push notifications: Notifications are delivered through Apple's Push Notification service, and to be useful the alert text contains a short preview of the actual content — the first line of a reply, an email's sender and subject, or a booking's route and price. That preview passes through Apple's servers and appears on your lock screen. You can hide previews in iOS Settings, or turn notifications off entirely.
  • Never sold, never used for ads, never used for training: We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.
  • Automated actions: osmos may draft or suggest actions (e.g. reply to an email, approve a payment). Actions with significant consequences ask for your confirmation by default. You can give standing permission in advance — marking a tool "always allow" in the Tool Manager, turning on auto-booking within limits you set, or starting an email errand that osmos then carries on by itself — and once you do, those actions go ahead without asking again.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Third-party service providers: We share data with vendors who help us operate the Services — cloud hosting and databases, AI inference, the integration partner that brokers your connected accounts, email delivery, product analytics, payment and subscription processing, travel booking partners, web search, maps, and sandboxed code execution. These providers are contractually required to use your data only on our behalf and in accordance with this policy. A current list of subprocessor categories is available on request.
  • Apple: Push notifications are delivered through Apple's servers, and the alert text includes a short preview of the content (see section 4).
  • Travel suppliers: When you book travel, we send the carrier, hotel or booking partner the passenger details they require to issue it — name, contact details, and where the operator requires it, date of birth and passport number.
  • Connected integrations: When you instruct osmos to take an action (e.g. send an email, post a Slack message), the relevant data is transmitted to the corresponding third-party service through their API.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change.

6. Data Retention

We keep different things for different lengths of time.

Deleted automatically: cached message bodies from a connected mailbox (days); WhatsApp message content (7 days); cached calendar events (90 days); mirrored email details such as sender, subject and preview (12 months); and expired travel price quotes (24 hours).

Kept for as long as your account exists: the things osmos creates with and for you — your chats, AI memory, tasks, routines, notifications, briefings, the tickets and bookings it extracts, your travel profile, your sent mail, your WhatsApp contact list, and all mail sent to and from your own osmos address — for that address we are the mailbox, not a cache, so there is no other copy and we do not expire it. These are the product: deleting them on a timer would take away what you came for. You can delete individual items in the App, or everything at once by deleting your account.

When you disconnect an integration, we stop accessing new data from that service and delete what it had cached. WhatsApp is the exception: messages expire on their usual seven-day cycle and the contact list stays until you delete your account.

You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → Delete Account). This immediately and permanently deletes your data from our systems — including chats, AI memory, extracted tickets and uploads — and deletes the access tokens we hold for your connected accounts. We also ask our providers to erase what they hold: your connected-app authorisations, your AI memory bank, your WhatsApp session, your osmos email address and your uploaded files. If one of those providers is unreachable at that moment we complete your deletion anyway and record the failure so we can finish the job.

Three things do not disappear. Your billing and subscription records stay with Apple and our payment providers, because we are required to keep financial records. If you opened an osmos wallet, the blockchain account itself is public and outside our control. And backups made before you deleted your account age out on their own retention cycle rather than being edited.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your data: encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256), application-layer encryption with per-user keys for sensitive fields (such as travel documents, connected-account credentials, and cached message content — a set we are actively expanding), strict access controls, and internal security review of our systems. Where a mailbox or calendar is connected through our integration partner, the authorisation stays with them and never reaches us. Where osmos connects to Gmail or Outlook directly, we hold the authorisation ourselves, encrypted at the application layer with a key unique to your account and decrypted only for the moments a request needs it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you become aware of a security vulnerability, please contact us immediately at help@meetosmos.com.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access and portability: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Restriction and objection: Request that we restrict processing or object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Disconnect integrations: You can revoke access to any connected service at any time through the Tool Manager in the App.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at help@meetosmos.com. We will respond within 30 days (or as required by applicable law).

9. International Data Transfers

To provide the Services, your personal data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside the United Kingdom, including the United States, where our AI infrastructure and certain cloud service providers are based.

9.1 UK–US Data Bridge

Where we transfer personal data from the UK to the United States, we rely on the UK–US Data Bridge (the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) where our US-based service providers are certified under the Data Privacy Framework with the UK Extension. We verify each provider's active certification status on the Data Privacy Framework List (dataprivacyframework.gov) and monitor it on an ongoing basis.

9.2 Alternative Safeguards

Where a US-based provider is not certified under the Data Privacy Framework, or where we transfer data to recipients in other countries outside the UK, we put in place appropriate safeguards as required by UK GDPR, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) or the UK Addendum to EU Standard Contractual Clauses. These safeguards ensure that your personal data receives a level of protection equivalent to that afforded under UK data protection law.

9.3 Your rights regarding international transfers

You have the right to request details of the safeguards we have in place for any specific international transfer of your personal data. To make such a request, please contact us at help@meetosmos.com.

10. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to individuals under the age of 16. When you create an account you confirm that you meet that age requirement; we do not ask for your date of birth and we do not otherwise verify it. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 16, we will take steps to delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at help@meetosmos.com.

11. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with third-party services (e.g. Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack). Your use of these services is governed by their own privacy policies and terms of service. We encourage you to review the privacy practices of each service you connect. osmos is not responsible for the data practices of third-party services.

12. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use a mobile analytics SDK (PostHog, hosted in the EU) to record usage events — feature usage, screen views, and technical metadata — for performance monitoring and improvement. These events are linked to your account. They never include the contents of your messages, emails, or documents.

Your continued use of the Services after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
